# Venva Instagram style guide

Brand: Venva — "Your body, explained." Science-backed health guidance for an Indian audience.
Pillars: Training · Nutrition · Sleep · Symptoms · Wellbeing. Site: venva.co.in. Handle: @venva.health.

## Voice
- Plain, confident, direct. Short sentences. No hype, no fear-mongering, no "miracle" claims.
- Indian context: Indian foods (dal, paneer, besan chilla, chai, roti), Indian health patterns
  (thin-fat / visceral fat, vitamin D & B12 deficiency, high salt), metric units, kg.
- Every number must be a real, widely accepted figure (WHO, ICMR, major meta-analyses). If unsure, don't use a number.
- Never diagnose or prescribe medication. Suggest "get tested" / "talk to a doctor" where relevant.

## Templates (image text — keep it SHORT, the image must be readable in 2 seconds)
1. `myth` — dark green card. Fields:
   - `label`: one of "Myth buster" | "Reality check" | "Did you know"
   - `headline`: 2–5 words, max ~22 characters per line, use \n to break into 2 lines. Usually a question (myths) or a punchy statement.
   - `body`: "The truth" — 20–28 words, 2–3 sentences (must fit in 4 lines).
2. `stat` — dark green card, one big number. Fields:
   - `number`: very short, e.g. "2.2L", "7-9", "5 g", "150 min" (max 7 characters)
   - `label`: 2–4 words, e.g. "Water per day"
   - `body`: 4 short lines separated by \n, each ≤ 38 characters.
3. `tip` — light mint card. Fields:
   - `label`: pillar name, e.g. "Nutrition", "Training", "Wellbeing", "Sleep", "Symptoms"
   - `headline`: 2–5 words, \n between 2 lines, ≤ 16 characters per line
   - `highlight`: the key takeaway, ≤ 28 characters (e.g. "Aim 25-35 g per meal")
   - EITHER `body` (18–24 words, must fit in 4 lines) OR `sections` (exactly 3 items of {title, text}, text ≤ 2 short lines)

## Caption formula
1. Hook line that names the problem (1 line). Optional emoji at most once.
2. Blank line, then a short punchy follow-up line.
3. The explanation / target, with "At 70 kg → ..." style concrete examples when useful.
4. A list of 3–5 items, each line starting with "→ ".
5. One-line takeaway.
6. "Full guide → venva.co.in"
7. "Save this 🔖 and follow @venva.health"
8. 5 lowercase hashtags on the last line, e.g. #nutrition #protein #fitness #health #india

## Weekly rotation (keeps the grid varied)
Mon tip · Tue myth (Myth buster) · Wed stat · Thu myth (Reality check) · Fri tip · Sat myth (Did you know) · Sun stat
